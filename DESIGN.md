# Design Direction

Clean, calm student dashboard. Off-white background, white rounded panels, deep navy typography, indigo accents, clear spacing, and readable system fonts. Top: headline and three summary cards. Middle: compact form. Bottom: seven-day bar chart and history. Mobile: stack cards and form fields. Empty state uses friendly guidance; invalid input appears inline in red; populated state highlights scores; reset returns to empty.

Note: The assignment also calls for design-direction trial pages before final styling; document your own design trials/decisions before submission.
