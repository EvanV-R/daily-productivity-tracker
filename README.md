# Daily Productivity Tracker

A small JavaScript app that saves daily habits, calculates a motivational productivity score, and shows weekly progress.

## How to use
Open the [live site](https://evanv-r.github.io/daily-productivity-tracker/). Enter a date, study hours, sleep hours, exercise minutes, and recreational screen hours. Save the day to view your score. Sort or delete entries, or reset the tracker. Data is saved only in your browser.

## What the app remembers
A dated list of habit entries and scores in localStorage. The latest score, 7-day average, chart, and history are derived from those entries.
